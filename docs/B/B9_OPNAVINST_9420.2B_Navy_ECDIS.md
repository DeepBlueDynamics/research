# 1. <u>Purpose</u> 

a. To provide Navy policy and guidance governing the implementation and operational use of the Navy Electronic Chart Display and Information System (ECDIS), commercial ECDIS and Electronic Chart Systems (ECS). 

b. To direct and guide the Navy’s transition from paper nautical charts to an electronic charting environment for primary navigation and piloting. 

c. To establish the minimum performance standards and the certification process for Navy ECDIS and ECS.

# 2. <u>Cancellation.  OPNAVINST 9420.2A.</u> 

3. <u>Scope and Applicability.  This policy applies to all U.S. Navy commissioned vessels, non-</u> commissioned craft (e.g., United States naval ships, amphibious warfare type craft and expeditionary craft) and organizations that support the development, acquisition, testing, installation, training, certification, operation and sustainment of Navy ECDIS, commercial ECDIS and ECS. 

# 4. <u>Background</u> 

a. Per references (a) and (b), the initial Navy implementation of ECDIS (i.e., ECDIS-N) has been implemented on the majority of U.S. Navy commissioned ships and submarines.  A revision to the current Navy ECDIS policy guidance is warranted to capitalize on the lessons learned from ECDIS-N implementation, exploit advancements in ECDIS technology, further clarify commercial ECDIS and ECS requirements for non-commissioned platforms, standardize ECDIS acquisition across the fleet and maintain interoperability with allied navies. 

b. The National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA) is transitioning away from Digital Navigation Charts (DNC) that are unique to the U. S. Navy and adopting international Electronic Navigation Chart (ENC) standards.  Additionally, NGA and other foreign Hydrographic Offices are in-progress or have planned cancellations of their Standard Nautical Chart (i.e., paper chart) portfolio which requires the Navy to fully transition to electronic navigation. 

c. The Navy ECDIS Program of Record (POR) was established by Program Executive Office, Integrated Warfare Systems (PEO IWS) to reduce the proliferation of ECDIS hardware and software versions and resultant training and sustainment challenges and to standardize implementation of the next generation of Navy ECDIS across the commissioned fleet.  The original implementation of ECDIS (ECDIS-N) will be replaced by the POR Navy ECDIS application software.

(3) Minimum performance standards for Navy ECDIS: 

(a) ECDIS performance standards identified in reference (c). 

(b) Warship ECDIS software performance standards identified in annex A of reference (d). 

(c) Process and display ENC and submarine electronic navigational charts (SMENC) in the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO) standards for the S-57 and future S-100 data format (when available) series. 

(d) Process and display vector product format (VPF) electronic charts and data from the NGA (e.g., DNC, tactical ocean data (TOD), VPF database update and the geospatial symbols for digital displays).  These performance standards will be canceled once NGA officially completes production and dissemination of the global portfolio of ENC required by the Services and CCMDs and all commissioned vessels have an ENC-compliant version of ECDIS. 

(e) Process, display, transfer and interrogate additional military layers (AML) containing specific military information (e.g., maritime boundaries, Q-routes, Operational Area (OPAREA)). 

(f) Accept and display surface search navigation radar target and video overlay, visual and other navigation fix information (e.g., Automatic Identification System). 

(g) An independent backup ECDIS matching the platform’s ECDIS version, as approved by OPNAV N2N6E, must be provided to ensure safe navigation in the event of primary ECDIS failure. 

(h) Display, process and store classified data on all consoles commensurate with the mission security requirements of the host vessel.

# b. <u>Military Sealift Command (MSC)</u> 

(1) MSC government-owned ships will utilize ECDIS compliant with International Maritime Organization (IMO) performance standards established for civil shipping per reference (c). 

(2) MSC vessels of appropriate tonnage may utilize ECS under applicable Federal Regulations. 

c. <u>Naval expeditionary craft</u> 

(1) All non-commissioned amphibious warfare type craft and Navy expeditionary craft identified in reference (e) must be able to safely navigate using an ECS that meets the performance standards in subparagraph 5c(2) and can process and display electronic charts provided by NGA without reliance on paper charts as a backup.  Applicable OPNAV Resource Sponsor and PEO Program Office will provide concurrence of craft type in reference (e). 

(2) Minimum performance standards for Navy ECS: 

(a) Process and display ENC in the IHO standards for the S-57 and S-100 data format (when available) series. 

(b) Process, display, transfer and interrogate AML containing specific military information (e.g., maritime boundaries, Q-routes, OPAREAs). 

(c) Craft that are employed in any mission in which direct action with an adversary is reasonably expected are required to employ both a primary and an independent secondary ECS to ensure safe navigation. 

(d) Craft that are employed in any mission in which direct action with an adversary is unlikely are only required to employ a primary ECS.

# <u>NAVY COMMISSIONED VESSEL CERIFICATION FOR UNRESTRICTED OPERATIONS TWO PHASE PROCESS</u> 

1. <u>Phase I (blocks 1 through 4).  Describes the process required to test, evaluate and approve the</u> Navy ECDIS configuration for the intended host platform(s). 

a. <u>Block 1.  At the beginning of phase I (Navy ECDIS configuration approval), candidate</u> Navy ECDIS configuration(s) will be identified to OPNAV N2N6E by the OPNAV platform resource sponsor.  Configuration(s) will be identified by ship class or submarine class; installed navigation equipment (controls and sensors, navigation data distribution and electronic charting processing and display); power distribution; electronic charting software; and ship control application planned for installation. 

b. <u>Block 2.  OPNAV N2N6E must convene a Navy ECDIS IPT for each candidate Navy</u> ECDIS configuration.  At least 1 month prior to convening the IPT, members will be provided the necessary information from the requesting organization to formulate a recommended test strategy.  Information must include, at a minimum, a description of the unapproved configuration; relevant test history; proposed test strategy; and concept of operations describing the Navy ECDIS hardware and software configuration and system architecture.  Test strategy must ensure configuration is adequate to support robustness, survivability and the environmental qualifications commensurate with platform requirements to accomplish ship’s mission(s). Additionally, test strategy should consider the manpower, personnel, training, maintenance and logistic infrastructure available to support this plan.  Each member of the Navy ECDIS IPT must review the documentation and recommendations for the Navy ECDIS candidate configuration to determine if additional testing NAVCERT assessment, OPTEVFOR, etc.) is required; or provide concurrence on presented test approach to obtain configuration approval; or provide justification for deviation from briefed proposals. 

c. <u>Block 3.  Each member of the Navy ECDIS IPT must review the documentation and</u> provide a recommendation for configuration approval.  Following consensus by the ECDIS IPT, recommendation for or against configuration approval, along with any requests for deviations, must be forwarded to OPNAV N2N6E for consideration.  If concurrence on the implementation details of the selected option cannot be achieved, OPNAV N2N6E will make a decision on the issues for which the IPT members cannot reach agreement.  In the event an OT is part of the recommended test strategy, OPTEVFOR will specify the testing required to assess the cyber survivability, operational effectiveness and operational suitability of the candidate Navy ECDIS configuration. 

d. <u>Block 4.  OPNAV N2N6E must review the recommendation(s) and issue a message</u> approving the Navy ECDIS configuration or direct corrective action and additional testing, as appropriate.

2. <u>Phase II (blocks 5 through 8).  Lists the systems command and fleet processes required to</u> properly install, test, certify and ultimately authorize the ship to navigate solely using the Navy ECDIS configuration. 

a. <u>Block 5.  At the beginning of phase II (shipboard ECDIS and crew certification), each</u> approved Navy ECDIS configuration installed aboard a ship or submarine must complete a system operational verification test per COMNAVSEASYSCOM technical specifications. System operational verification test ensures system hardware and software work properly and that interface data is received into the system in the proper format. 

b. <u>Block 6.  To ensure system hardware, software and interfaces work properly in the new</u> configuration, a NAVCERT must be conducted per reference (g). 

c. <u>Block 7.  After installation of an approved configuration, and completion of the</u> NAVCERT, individual ships and submarines must successfully complete a Navy ECDIS crew certification, per guidance issued by the respective fleet commander and TYCOM. 

d. <u>Block 8.  Upon successful completion of the Navy ECDIS crew certification, the</u> TYCOM will authorize the ship or submarine for unrestricted navigation operations using Navy ECDIS as the primary official navigation plot.

# <u>NAVY NON-COMMISSIONED CRAFT ELECTRONIC CHART SYSTEMS (ECS) UNRESTRICTED OPERATIONS PROCESS</u> 

1. <u>Block 1.  The ECS installed on non-commissioned craft identified in reference (e) will be</u> selected by the PEO Program Office responsible for procuring the ECS on the specific noncommissioned craft.  The ECS selected for non-commissioned craft must meet the minimum standards identified in this instruction.  The procuring PEO Program office should seek concurrence that the ECS is suitable and effective for the craft type from the TYCOM and applicable OPNAV Resource Sponsor. 

2. <u>Block 2.  Each approved ECS installed aboard a non-commissioned craft identified in</u> reference (e) must successfully complete a joint materiel readiness inspection per references (h) and (i) conducted by the installing PEO Program Office and the TYCOM to ensure ECS operability. 

3. <u>Block 3.  After successful installation and PEO Program Office’s TYCOM inspection, the</u> individual non-commissioned craft must complete a successful ECS TYCOM conducted crew certification and navigation assessment that verifies personnel operating the ECS can safely navigate the non-commissioned craft per current TYCOM navigation policy, doctrine and standard operating procedures. 

4. <u>Block 4.  Upon successful completion of the ECS crew certification and navigation</u> assessment, the TYCOM will authorize the non-commissioned craft for unrestricted navigation operations using the installed ECS as the primary official navigation plot.
