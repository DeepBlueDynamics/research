# 1.4 Defense Need (labelled working copy)

Not for Volume 2 assembly. Clean filed text is `1_4_defense_need.md`.

**Labels:** **S** = topic/Q&A or cited Government document; **P** = proposed response; **A** = unverified. Naming a transition target is **not** an endorsement, funded integration, or committed customer.

**Transition [S].** Q&A names the GPNTS program of record as transition owner, Phase II ICDs as GFI, no shore interface, Phase I on synthetic ASPN/pntOS. Intended path **[P]**; not a claim that GPNTS selected this firm.

**Use case [S].** Destroyer transit through a GPS-degraded or spoofed strait. User: electronics technician–navigation (ETV). ECDIS-like layout is a familiarity target, not certification.

**Differentiation [S need / P hypothesis].** Fragmented physical displays increase workload and slow decisions. The increment is one workflow for triage (fault/jam/spoof) and fallback: a unified air-gapped informational console (source health, transparent composite, named missing evidence) with no new sensors, ownship-fix algorithms, or ship control. No operator-performance gain is claimed until measured.

**Need remains [S].** OPNAVINST 9420.1C ¶5a(3) requires a DoD-approved primary and a GPS-independent alternate; ¶5a(11) requires indicating degradation from jamming, multipath, weather, terrain, or spoofing. Exhibit P-40, OPN LI 2657 (Mar 2024) describes GPNTS as the Navy primary PNT system (open architecture, MGUE M-code); FY2025 M-GUE and NoGAPSS kits install in FY2026 (secondary copy of the unclassified exhibit). GAO-22-106010 (5 Aug 2022) found incomplete Navy alternative-PNT business cases and no Oversight Council progress metrics.

**Currency [S].** NAVCEN closed marine reports Jun–Aug 2026: Red Sea jamming/spoofing, Fujairah multi-vessel disruption, Baltic interference, GPS failure SE of Sweden; no constellation anomaly.

**Increment [P].** Phase I synthetic feasibility versus a fragmented-display surrogate (test control, not a fleet baseline). Phase II modular GPNTS mapping when ICDs arrive. Phase III dual-use per the topic’s Phase III list (examples: commercial shipping and logistics, aviation operations, critical infrastructure monitoring).
