## Final agreement

The product is a unified APNT awareness and decision-support interface. The simulator tests unsupported confidence as well as useful decisions; it is not a substitute navigator or the proposed operational product.

Fixed constraints: full-stack airgap, secured runtime, and strictly less than1second from input receipt to the displayed alert and initial informational recommendation. Any ≥1second event fails the tested envelope. Security controls stay enabled during timing tests; the initial path survives local-model failure. No percentile waiver.

Freeze the observation model, acceptable response sets, critical cases, threat/control checklist and hardware/load profile before tuning.60holdout cases are a coverage budget, not proof of fleet reliability. Nominal/recoverable usefulness gates do not waive critical-case or unresolved-evidence failures. No invented baseline values, calibration claim, IRB exemption or accreditation.
