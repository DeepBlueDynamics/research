## 1. Identification and Significance of the Problem / Operational Need

The Navy does not lack positioning, navigation, and timing (PNT) sources. It lacks a unified way for the electronics technician–navigation (ETV) to understand them. As additional Assured PNT (APNT) systems are fielded, operators consult multiple interfaces for health, confidence, risk, and recovery. The topic states that this fragmentation increases cognitive workload, complicates training, and slows decision-making [T]. The user is an ETV, manned for navigation issues or contested transit and most familiar with Navy-standard Electronic Chart Display and Information System (ECDIS) type displays [QA-808]. Phase I targets shipboard GPS-Based Positioning, Navigation, and Timing Service (GPNTS) ingest via All-Source Positioning and Navigation (ASPN), the PNT operating system (pntOS), or other message standards; shore and mission-support interfaces are out of scope [QA-826]. ECDIS similarity is a user-experience familiarity target, not a Phase I S-57/S-52/NMEA certification requirement [QA-828].

This is a software, data-integration, and human-machine-interface problem. The topic does not seek new PNT or APNT sensing, navigation algorithms, timing sources, or hardware [T]. Navy policy requires a Department of Defense–approved primary (GPS), at least one independent alternate, and indication of degradation from interference including jamming and spoofing [OPNAV 5.a.(3), 5.a.(11)]. The Government Accountability Office’s 2021 assessment records DoD’s plan to keep GPS at the core while complementary sources check one another when quality degrades, and that no single PNT system supports all DoD requirements [GAO-21-320SP]. NIST IR 8323r1 and the DHS Resilient PNT Conformance Framework (v2.0) treat detection of disruption or manipulation, and recovery including common-mode failure, as responsible-use functions [NIST, DHS-CF]. Joint All-Domain Command and Control (JADC2) sense / make-sense / act in contested electromagnetic environments is the joint context [JADC2]. These documents are not GPNTS interface specifications; they establish why multi-source operator awareness is a defense need.

GNSS disruption is an operational maritime condition. Maritime Administration Advisory 2026-008 (active; expires 21 October 2026) states that GPS disruption affects maritime navigation worldwide, directs reporting to the U.S. Coast Guard Navigation Center (NAVCEN), and warns that Automatic Identification System transmissions are unencrypted and spoofable and must never be the sole basis for navigational decisions [MARAD]. Closed June–August 2026 NAVCEN marine reports from the Baltic, Fujairah (including “all the vessels”), and the Red Sea record interference or spoofing with no GPS constellation or control-segment anomaly [NAVCEN]. Commercial Hormuz or Baltic event counts are not used as Navy-validated statistics.

The load-bearing difficulty is decision under uncertainty, not display of a single “correct” fix. Progressive degradation yields at least three evidence states: sources disagree; correlated sources agree while both are wrong; remaining independent evidence cannot justify a unique recovery action. Forcing a single course of action (COA) in the last case trains overconfidence. Permanent abstention fails the recoverable cases the Q&A names as most valuable: distinguishing hardware fault, jamming, and spoofing; selecting an alternate PNT source; confirming inertial-navigation-system (INS) only operation; and initiating degraded operations [QA-821, QA-825]. Phase I recommendations remain informational; the operator retains decision authority [QA-701]. Broader mission-phase data are not planned for Phase I ingestion [QA-819, QA-826].

**Proposal hypothesis (not a Navy requirement).** Feasibility will be scored with a Kobayashi Maru-style evaluation of problem-solving. Under progressive PNT degradation the interface must state what is known at source and composite levels, expose failed assumptions and dependencies, and name independent evidence that would resolve remaining ambiguity. Labeled no-win cases must permit an INDETERMINATE state rather than a forced COA. Nominal and recoverable cases are included so permanent abstention cannot score well. Scenarios are seeded and reproducible; ground truth is held separate from system inputs; a subset is held out; the fragmented-display baseline and the prototype receive identical inputs. The product is the unified APNT operator-awareness interface. The scenario driver is evaluation infrastructure, not a Navy trainer, new navigator, or ship-control loop. The entire stack—ingest, analytics, any AI inference, and UI—runs locally with no remote dependency [QA-820]. Sub-second ingest through rendered alert and initial COA is a hard gate; detailed rationale stays asynchronous [QA-825].

## 2. Phase I Technical Objectives & Key Performance Parameters (KPPs)

Phase I establishes feasibility of a unified operator interface—not a new navigator—using representative synthetic data that conforms to ASPN/pntOS [QA-825, QA-828]. The offeror will adapt a commercially derived data-integration and decision-support platform, an approach the Q&A confirms is responsive [QA-831]. End-of-Phase-I maturity is design concepts, feasibility evidence, and a low-to-medium-fidelity demonstration on simulated data [QA-808]. No live GPNTS integration, no human research, no new sensors or navigation algorithms, and no autonomous ship control [T, QA-901, QA-808]. The 28 August answer states that all Phase I data and activity are controlled unclassified information (CUI) while allowing open-standard synthetic data; a synthetic demonstration is not treated as implying unrestricted or public performance, and handling/marking obligations remain to be clarified with the Government [QA-828]. ECDIS resemblance is a UX familiarity target [QA-828].

**O1 — Ingest and display without collapse.** Normalize three to eight simultaneous PNT sources at 1 Hz to 10 Hz and faithfully visualize source-level health and confidence without collapsing source state into the composite [QA-820, QA-825].

**O2 — Composite with explanation.** Derive a correlated composite confidence using transparent rules (acceptable for Phase I). More advanced methods only if explainable and runnable in a constrained, air-gapped edge environment [QA-825]. Present the minimum subset first; full evidence on operator drill-down [QA-820].

**O3 — Hard sub-second hot path.** Every labeled onset: ingest through rendered alert and initial informational COA in < 1.0 s. No percentile miss allowance. Detailed rationale remains asynchronous [QA-825].

**O4 — Decision-under-uncertainty evaluation.** Freeze a reproducible scenario library with nominal, recoverable, progressive-degradation, and no-win (Kobayashi Maru) families; score against held-out labels without forcing a unique COA when independent evidence is insufficient (**P**, not a Navy-stated KPP).

**O5 — Air-gapped secured runtime.** Entire stack—including analytics, any local AI, and UI—runs isolated with no remote Navy data center, shore, or cloud dependency [QA-820]. Phase I verifies a secured-runtime gate (least privilege, controlled access, pinned/scanned dependencies, protected data and configuration, audit). Passing the gate is **not** an accreditation, ATO, or CMMC-status claim. Iron Bank remains a Phase II path; the Q&A did not require an Iron Bank base image in Phase I [QA-808, QA-828].

Preferred Government metrics named in Q&A are speed to decision during an anomaly, threat-comprehension accuracy, decision accuracy (correct fallback), and reduced cognitive workload, against a baseline of monitoring multiple fragmented physical displays [QA-808, QA-820, QA-825]. Phase I will **instrument** those quantities on the demonstration; it will **not** claim pre-measured percentage improvements, Government acceptance thresholds, or operator-endorsed workload reduction. Given the 1 September answer that no human research is expected, NASA-TLX / SUS / SAGAT participant studies are **not** Phase I commitments [QA-901]. Workload is treated as a Phase II measurement question pending Government confirmation of acceptable non-research evaluation activity.

**Measurement plan (denominator).** After the scenario library is frozen, N is frozen. Proposed starting composition (**P**, not a Government sample-size requirement): 6 nominal, 8 recoverable (fault / jam / spoof with independent evidence in the inputs), 6 progressive-degradation, 4 no-win = 24 scored runs, plus at least 4 held-out mixed cases. Identical scenario files drive the fragmented-display baseline and the prototype. Ground-truth labels live in a store the system under test cannot read.
