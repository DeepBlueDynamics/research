## Page 1. Identification and Significance of the Problem / Operational Need

**The operator problem.** During a destroyer transit through a strait where GPS is jammed or spoofed, the electronics technician (navigation) at the APNT station must answer three questions fast: what do we actually know about our position and time, which assumption just failed, and what independent evidence would settle it. Today that answer is assembled by reading several fragmented physical displays, each speaking for one source. The topic states the consequence plainly: the fragmented environment increases cognitive workload, complicates training, and slows decision-making, and the most valuable capability is faster anomaly triage (fault versus jamming versus spoofing) and fallback execution such as selecting an alternate source or confirming an INS-only transition [S1]. Navy policy already requires new PNT systems to indicate degradation to the operator due to jamming, multipath, weather, terrain, or spoofing [S5]. The gap is not another indicator. It is the absence of one place where indicators are composed, weighed, and turned into an informational recommendation with its evidence attached.

**The need is current and growing.** In the first 24 hours after hostilities began in the Gulf on 28 February 2026, a maritime-intelligence firm logged more than 1,100 vessels with GPS and AIS interference, with reported positions displaced onto an airport and a nuclear plant, and tanker transits through the Strait of Hormuz fell roughly 90 percent within a week [S9][S10][S11]. IMO, ICAO, and ITU jointly declared GNSS interference a grave concern in March 2025, after IMO's 2021 circular on deliberate GPS/GNSS interference [S12][S13]; U.S. Maritime Advisory 2026-008 tells mariners to plan for GPS disruption before getting underway [S14]. On the Navy side, GPNTS is the primary shipboard PNT system, is open-architecture, and is now fielding M-code and non-GPS-aided positioning upgrade kits, with FY2025 procurements installing in FY2026 [S8]. GAO reported that DOD will keep GPS at the core and complement it with relative sources whose errors accumulate and absolute sources that depend on external availability, and later that the Navy's alternative-PNT efforts lacked complete business cases and DOD lacked progress metrics [S6][S7]. Every added source is one more voice the operator must reconcile.

**Why this is a decision-under-uncertainty problem.** Existing standards define per-source integrity only. IMO's multi-system receiver standard requires an alert when the performance assessment cannot be determined and prescribes explicit "last valid fix" states so no ambiguity exists [S15]; its PNT data-processing guideline grades integrity from none through cross-source consistency to protection levels [S16]. The DHS resilience framework notes that detection is probabilistic, that thresholds trade false alarms against misses, and that an untrustworthy source is not always wrong [S18]. The load-bearing case is coherent regional bias: two GNSS receivers agree while both are wrong, so cross-receiver checks pass and only a comparison against an error-independent family (inertial dead reckoning, radar fix, clock holdover) exposes it [S26]. A console that forces a single confident course of action in that moment is worse than one that says "indeterminate; verify against radar fix" and shows why. JADC2 expects commanders to act with minimum guidance where sensing is degraded and adversary intent is ambiguous [S20].

**Proposal hypothesis, not a Navy requirement.** [Company] proposes to adapt its data-integration and decision-support software into a unified APNT awareness console for the ETV that (a) renders each source's own health and integrity without collapsing it, (b) derives a transparent composite confidence and triage class, (c) presents an informational initial course of action within a sub-second of ingestion with evidence available on drill-down, and (d) represents indeterminacy honestly, naming the evidence that would resolve it. We hypothesize this measurably improves triage correctness and time-to-actionable-information over fragmented per-source displays, without training operators toward permanent abstention. Feasibility will be tested with a "Kobayashi Maru" scenario family: seeded, reproducible progressive-degradation runs that include nominal, recoverable, and no-win cases, with ground truth held apart from system inputs, held-out cases never used for tuning, and identical inputs replayed to a fragmented baseline and the prototype. The simulator is evaluation infrastructure only; the product is the console.

**Boundaries.** No new sensors, navigation algorithms, timing sources, hardware, or autonomous ship control [S1]. Phase I aids are informational only; all rendering and analytics run locally and 100 percent air-gapped; the architecture is API-first for 3 to 8 sources at 1 to 10 Hz; data are synthetic and ASPN/pntOS-conformant; the ECDIS-like presentation is a familiarity target for the ETV, not a certification claim [S1]. The Government has stated Phase I data and activity are CUI; handling scope will be confirmed at kickoff, and synthetic data do not imply that results are publicly releasable [S1].

## Page 2. Phase I Technical Objectives & Key Performance Parameters (KPPs)

Each objective is written as the feasibility question Phase I must answer. Basis codes: **SR** = stated in the topic or Q&A; **PT** = our proposed measurable target; **UA** = unverified assumption we will confirm or replace.

**O1 Faithful normalization.** Can 3 to 8 disparate synthetic APNT sources (ASPN/pntOS-conformant, 1 to 10 Hz) be standardized for analytics and display without losing any source-provided health, integrity, or confidence field? (SR for the source envelope; PT for lossless rendering.)

**O2 Transparent composite and triage.** Does a transparent, explainable method (rules and statistics, no opaque model in the decision path) yield a composite confidence and a triage class in {nominal, fault, jam, spoof, indeterminate} that is correct and calibrated on held-out scenarios, including coherent regional bias?

**O3 Sub-second hot path with progressive disclosure.** Can ingestion → normalization → composite → triage → informational initial COA → render stay under one second at full load in a containerized, air-gapped runtime, with rationale and evidence served afterward on drill-down, and every decision written to a hash-chained, replayable trace?

**O4 Decision-uncertainty behavior (Kobayashi Maru family).** On no-win cases, does the console withhold a single confident COA and name the resolving evidence, while on nominal and recoverable cases it does not abstain? This is our hypothesis, evaluated by a scripted-policy harness, not by human-subject research.

**O5 Familiar UX and integration path.** Can every display element be mapped to an ECDIS/BAM convention or explicitly marked offeror-added, and can the GPNTS/ASPN/pntOS mapping be documented as a modular, API-first plan for Phase II?

Participant-based measures (speed-to-decision, workload, situation-awareness probes) are deferred to Phase II pending the Government's 1 September 2026 statement that no human research is expected [S1]; Phase I uses harness measurement and SME design review only.
