## Page 1 — 1. Identification and Significance of the Problem / Operational Need

An operator confronting conflicting navigation information needs more than another status indicator. The immediate questions are which information remains credible, what the disagreement means, and which response the available evidence supports. DON26BX05-NP004 identifies fragmented APNT interfaces as a source of workload and delayed decisions. It seeks a unified operator experience incorporating individual-source status and a correlated assessment, with GPNTS as the integration target. [1]

DeepBlue Dynamics proposes an APNT decision-support interface that makes both conclusions and their limits visible. Its distinguishing hypothesis is that **showing why a decision is supportable—and what evidence is missing when it is not—will make a unified display more useful than aggregation alone.** Phase I will establish technical feasibility and a defensible path to testing operator benefit. That benefit remains a hypothesis.

Consider a simulated destroyer transit through a degraded strait. Two positioning feeds agree, but their agreement reflects a shared dependency. A separate reference initially contradicts them, then becomes stale. A display that counts agreeing feeds can present apparent consensus while concealing the loss of independent corroboration. A display that merely declares uncertainty can also fail: it leaves the operator to reconstruct why confidence changed and whether any usable recovery option remains.

Our interface will preserve individual-source assessments, expose declared dependencies and evidence age, and explain the basis of its composite assessment. Informational action cards will identify their prerequisites. When those prerequisites cannot be established, the interface will name the missing evidence and present bounded verification or degraded-operation guidance. It will not invent a sensor, assert an unavailable observation, or characterize an uncalibrated score as a probability of safety.

The proposed evaluation deliberately includes situations where the observations cannot distinguish competing explanations. This changes the simulation's success criterion: a forced diagnosis is not always a correct answer. The system should recognize unresolved alternatives and show what accessible evidence could discriminate between them. When no such evidence is available, it should state that limit. Nominal and recoverable controls prevent an always-uncertain system from passing. In particular, two simulated worlds producing identical visible inputs must not elicit different diagnoses merely because the evaluator knows their different ground truths.

The product is the operational interface and its explainable integration service. The simulator supplies reproducible feasibility evidence. We will adapt the repository's retrieval foundation and interface concepts, keeping versioned analytical rules on the initial alert path and using local retrieval for supporting material. These assets are a starting point; the repository does not establish an integrated, validated APNT capability. ECDIS familiarity will guide information layout, with progressive disclosure to keep the initial view concise. [1]

The research risk is whether the proposed uncertainty treatment improves useful information access without masking actionable conditions or creating excessive alerts. A controlled comparison will separate the value of unified presentation from the added value of dependency and prerequisite explanations. Phase I will deliver measured software behavior, documented failure cases and an operator-evaluation plan; it will not substitute simulation scores for demonstrated human performance.

## Page 2 — 2. Phase I Technical Objectives & Key Performance Parameters (KPPs)

The six-month base will test whether a modular, locally executable demonstration can present timely APNT assessments and defensible informational recommendations across nominal, recoverable and unresolved conditions. The source-stated design envelope is 3–8 sources at 1–10 Hz, entirely local execution, and less than one second from ingestion to displayed alert and initial recommendation. Detailed evidence may follow through drill-down. [1] The targets below are offeror-proposed feasibility gates, not Government acceptance thresholds.

**Objective 1: Integrate and preserve evidence.** Pin public ASPN/pntOS interface assumptions, validate synthetic inputs, preserve source identity, timestamps and provided confidence, and publish a composite assessment with explicit dependency and freshness rules. Unavailable metadata remains unavailable; it is not silently inferred.

**Objective 2: Explain actionable and unresolved states.** Implement versioned informational recommendation rules with prerequisites and evidence links. Keep the initial alert path independent of model generation. Distinguish absent corroboration from a proven fault, and retain alternatives that the observations cannot separate.

**Objective 3: Demonstrate reproducible feasibility.** Before evaluation, freeze a proposed 60-case holdout set: 20 nominal, 20 recoverable and 20 unresolved cases, including common dependencies, missing metadata and stale inputs. Freeze acceptable recommendation sets and prohibited claims from a documented observation model; simulator truth is evaluator-only. Development cases are separate. Compare a fragmented-display surrogate, unified presentation alone, and the full explanatory interface using identical available inputs.

| KPP / proposed gate | Measurement and failure condition |
|---|---|
| Timely presentation | At 3 and 8 sources, each at 1 and 10 Hz, run four 30-minute load tests with injected events. Timestamp ingress and actual rendered alert/initial recommendation with a monotonic clock; report every event, maximum, p50/p95/p99 and drops. Any missing event or latency ≥1 s fails the tested envelope. Declare hardware, software and event counts. |
| Evidence preservation | All 60 holdout cases must retain required source fields or display their absence, and link every composite assessment/recommendation to input IDs and rule version. Any silent loss or unsupported evidence link fails. |
| Appropriate uncertainty | Zero unsupported definitive diagnoses or prerequisite-violating recommendations across the 20 unresolved cases. Each must identify the missing discriminating evidence or state that none is accessible. Report cases and recommendation counts; one violation fails. |
| Useful decisiveness | In at least 18/20 nominal and 18/20 recoverable cases, present a recommendation in the predeclared acceptable set. Blanket abstention fails; report both strata separately and every failure. |
| Isolation and repeatability | Complete the holdout suite with external networking blocked and dependencies preloaded. Three repeats must reproduce the same analytical states and recommendation IDs, excluding timestamps. Any remote dependency, truth leakage or unexplained analytical mismatch fails. |
| Secured runtime (user clarification) | Freeze the threat model and control checklist before testing. Require non-root services, least privilege, authenticated role-limited access, verified/pinned offline packages and SBOM, vulnerability disposition, protected stored data/keys, and audit of access/configuration changes. Negative tests must deny unauthorized access and modified packages; inspect effective runtime controls and audit persistence. Any failed mandatory control or unresolved critical/high finding fails the demonstration gate; document limits separately from formal authorization. |
| Information accessibility | For 12 predefined evidence-finding tasks, record required views and interactions across all three interfaces. Proposed gate: no task loses required evidence and at least 9/12 require fewer view changes than the fragmented surrogate. These are workflow proxies, not measured workload or decision-time improvements. |

Failed gates will be reported and used to bound Phase II claims. The final report will include configurations, case-level outcomes, traceable defects, integration risks and a plan to measure operator comprehension, decisions and workload. Participant research is not included in this base evaluation; the topic's September 1 answer and the appropriate determination process must govern any later participant activity. [1]
